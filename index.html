GCC-TBC Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Understand how marks are distributed across all three components of the MSCE Pune GCC-TBC examination. You must clear the passing threshold in each individual section.

Paper 1 · 40 Marks

Speed Passage Test

Candidates must reproduce a standard passage (300 words for 30 WPM, 400 words for 40 WPM, 500 words for 50 WPM) within 10 minutes. 1 mark is deducted for each error.

Pass Threshold: 16/40 (40%) Duration: 10:00 mins
Paper 2 · 25/50 Marks

Objective Theory MCQs

25 multiple-choice questions covering Computer Fundamentals, Windows OS, MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, and Internet/Email applications.

Pass Threshold: 40% (10/25) Duration: 25:00 mins
Paper 3 · 5 Marks

Official Email Drafting

Candidates are given a reference communication and must reproduce the recipient To address, Subject line, Body text, and select the correct Attachment file.

Pass Threshold: 2/5 (40%) Duration: 05:00 mins

Frequently Asked Questions
What is GCC-TBC and who conducts it?

GCC-TBC stands for Government Certificate in Computer Typing Basic Course. It is the official certification exam conducted by the Maharashtra State Council of Examination (MSCE), Pune. It is mandatory for clerical and typist appointments across government departments in Maharashtra and Goa.

What is the passing criterion for GCC-TBC typing?

Candidates must score at least 16 out of 40 marks (40%) in the Speed Passage, 2 out of 5 marks (40%) in Email Drafting, and 40% in the Objective MCQ paper. Scoring below 40% in any individual section results in a fail.

How is typing speed (WPM) scored and penalized?

In the official 10-minute speed test, Gross WPM is based on total characters typed. Net WPM is calculated after deducting penalties for every full mistake (omission, substitution, addition, spelling, spacing error, or missing paragraph Tab indent). Each mistake costs 1 mark out of 40.

Why must I press Tab at the beginning of each paragraph?

GCC-TBC exam software requires candidates to start every paragraph with a Tab indent. If you type spaces instead or begin typing immediately without pressing Tab, a 1-mark indent penalty is deducted for that paragraph.

Can I practice on an Android phone or tablet?

Yes. This platform works on all modern smartphone browsers. To build muscle memory for the real exam, connect a physical USB keyboard using an OTG adapter (or a Bluetooth keyboard) to your phone or tablet.
