Tips & articles

5 ways to increase your typing speed

Keep your fingers on the home row, look at the screen instead of the keyboard, start with accuracy before chasing speed, learn common word patterns, and time short daily drills instead of long occasional ones.

QWERTY vs Dvorak: does layout matter?

QWERTY remains the global standard and the one every exam and keyboard expects, so it's the most practical layout to master even though alternatives like Dvorak claim minor efficiency gains.

Government typing exam: what to expect

Most govt. typing tests are timed, scored on net WPM after error deductions, and require you to reproduce a passage exactly — including punctuation, spacing and capitalisation, which is exactly what this test practices.

Frequently asked questions
What is a good typing speed in WPM?

For most everyday and office work, 35-45 WPM is considered good, while 60+ WPM is fast. Government typing exams in India usually require 30-35 WPM with high accuracy.

How is typing speed (WPM) calculated?

WPM is calculated by dividing the number of words typed by the time taken in minutes. Net WPM also subtracts a penalty for mistakes, which is how most government exams score candidates.

What is the passing WPM for government typing exams?

Most Indian government typing exams require a minimum of 30-35 WPM in English with limited errors, though exact cutoffs vary by department and post.

How can I improve my typing accuracy and speed?

Practice daily with proper finger placement on the home row, avoid looking at the keyboard, start slow and prioritise accuracy over speed, and gradually increase your pace as muscle memory builds.
