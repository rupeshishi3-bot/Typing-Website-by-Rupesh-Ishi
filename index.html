Frequently Asked Questions & Platform Guide
What makes Rupesh Ishi Typing unique compared to other platforms?

This platform merges the best aspects of Monkeytype (minimalist, distraction-free speed tests, customizable time and word limits, live WPM, smooth caret, instant restart) and TypingClub (24-lesson guided touch typing academy, virtual keyboard, color-coded finger zones, and star ratings), together with an authentic MSCE Pune GCC-TBC Examination Simulator.

How is Net WPM calculated in the speed test?

Gross WPM is calculated as (Total Characters Typed / 5) / Elapsed Minutes. Net WPM deducts error penalties: (Correct Characters / 5) / Elapsed Minutes. In the GCC-TBC exam mode, each uncorrected typo or missing paragraph Tab indent deducts 1 mark from the 40-mark total.

Can I practice touch typing on my mobile phone?

Yes! The interface is fully responsive. For beginner lessons and exam preparation, connecting a physical USB or Bluetooth keyboard via an OTG adapter provides an optimal muscle-memory learning experience.

How does the Adaptive Learning Weak Keys feature work?

Every keystroke error is tracked by key. The platform highlights your 5 most error-prone keys and generates custom drill sentences packed with those specific characters so you can quickly eliminate bad muscle memory.
